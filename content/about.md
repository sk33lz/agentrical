I started Agentrical to help businesses and organizations build and deploy AI agents with better structure, better standards, better security, and ultimately better outcomes for your ROI in AI.

Before launching Agentrical, I spent years building and deploying production-ready websites and servers where quality, speed, and security all mattered at the same time. That experience now shapes how I design and deploy agent workflows for clients: clear scopes, measurable outputs, and security guardrails to protect your data and save costs.

If you are evaluating where agents can create real value in your business, I can help you define the right use cases and build the workflows that actually hold up in production.
