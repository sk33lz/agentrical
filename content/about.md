I started Agentrical to help businesses and organizations use AI agents with better structure, better standards, better security, and ultimately better outcomes.

Before launching Agentrical, I spent years building production-ready websites and systems where quality, speed, and accountability all mattered at the same time. That experience now shapes how I design and deploy agent workflows for clients: clear scopes, measurable outputs, and guardrails to protect your data and save costs.

If you are evaluating where agents can create real value in your business, I can help you define the right use cases and build the workflows that actually hold up in production.
